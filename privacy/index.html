Privacy policy

Last updated: September 2026

This page covers the apps, desklets, and applets I build and publish independently — listed below by name. I don't run any servers for these tools. Each one runs entirely on your own device, and any third-party account access (like Google) is used only to fetch data you explicitly connect, for local display purposes only. I don't collect, store, or transmit your personal data to myself or anyone else.

GCal Combined Desklet

Cinnamon desklet · Google Calendar & Tasks

What it does

A Cinnamon desktop desklet that displays your upcoming Google Calendar events and Google Tasks directly on your desktop, styled to match your Cinnamon theme.

Data it accesses

Calendar event titles Start/end times Locations Calendar colors Task titles Task due dates

The desklet reads this data via the official Google Calendar API and Google Tasks API, using OAuth credentials you generate and authorize yourself in your own Google Cloud project.

How it's used and stored

  • Data is fetched only to render it inside the desklet on your desktop — it is never sent anywhere else.
  • OAuth tokens and credentials are stored locally on your machine, at ~/.config/gcal-desklets, and never leave your device.
  • There is no backend server. I do not have access to your calendar, your tasks, or your tokens at any point.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or analytics.

Revoking access

You can revoke this app's access at any time from your Google Account permissions page, or by deleting the token file in ~/.config/gcal-desklets.
